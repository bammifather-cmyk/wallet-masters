No. You can pay by bank transfer in your local currency using Express Deposit, and the app converts it for you.

Yes. Tap “Open in browser”. The full platform runs there perfectly, and you can add it to your home screen.

Yes. OAT Trades grows by reference, so you register with the UID of the member who invited you. Ask them for it before you sign up.

Yes. The app loads the live platform, so new markets and features appear automatically without another download.
