Wallet Masters

Professional Crypto Wallet & Earning Platform

What is Wallet Masters?
Wallet Masters is a professional USDT crypto wallet built on the TRON (TRC20) blockchain. Users earn passive income hourly, connect earning apps, and withdraw funds to crypto wallets or local bank accounts (VIP members).
How Earnings Work
Standard members earn 50 USDT every hour. VIP members earn 200 USDT per hour. Simply claim your hourly bonus once per hour — earnings are credited instantly to your wallet balance.
SocialPay
Post pictures, voice messages, and stories. Admin reviews and sends likes to great posts. Earn 100 USDT for 1K likes, 1,000 USDT for 10K likes, 10,000 USDT for 100K likes, and up to 100,000 USDT for 1M likes!
Referral Program
Share your unique referral link and earn 200 USDT for every new user who joins Wallet Masters through your link.

Wallet Masters reserves the right to adjust fee rates with 7-day prior notice.

VIP members gain access to exclusive features including bank withdrawals, USDT Mining, and enhanced earning rates.

All SocialPay posts are subject to mandatory admin review before becoming publicly visible or earning-eligible. Content must be original, non-offensive, and appropriate for all audiences. 8. TP$ Earners Program
The TP$ (Thousands Per Second) Earners feature is available to users with a balance of 100,000 USDT or more. Earnings accumulate per tap with an increasing multiplier. Minimum 1,000 USDT must be accumulated before withdrawal to main balance. Abuse, automated tapping, or bot usage will result in immediate disqualification and account suspension.

13. USDT Mining
USDT Mining is a VIP-exclusive feature that allows users to earn passive returns on their balance. Users purchase mining hash starting from 1,000 USDT using their main balance. Each mining session lasts 1 hour and returns a 50% profit on the hash amount purchased (e.g., purchase 10,000 USDT hash → receive 15,000 USDT after 1 hour). After claiming profits, a 24-hour cooldown period applies before a new mining session can begin. Mining profits are credited directly to the user's main balance. The platform reserves the right to adjust profit rates, session durations, and cooldown periods. Mining hash purchases are non-refundable once a session has started. Users must have sufficient balance before initiating a mining session.
14. Internal Transfers
Users can send USDT to other Wallet Masters users instantly by entering the recipient's unique UID. Internal transfers are free of charge — no gateway fees apply. Transfers are processed immediately and cannot be reversed once completed. Both the sender and recipient receive a notification upon successful transfer. Users are responsible for verifying the recipient UID before sending. Wallet Masters is not liable for USDT sent to the wrong recipient due to user error. Minimum transfer amount: 1 USDT. Users cannot send transfers to their own account. All transfers are recorded in the transaction history of both parties.
15. Currency Converter
The currency converter tool allows users to view their USDT balance and enter custom amounts to see approximate values in their local currency. Exchange rates displayed are approximate reference rates and are for informational purposes only. The converter does not facilitate actual currency exchange transactions. Rates are updated periodically and may not reflect real-time market rates. Wallet Masters does not offer currency exchange services. Users should verify current rates through official financial sources before making decisions based on displayed conversions.
16. Referral Program
Users can share their unique referral link to invite new users to Wallet Masters. The referrer earns 200 USDT for each new user who successfully registers through their referral link. Referral bonuses are credited instantly to the referrer's balance upon successful registration of the referred user. A referral is only valid for new users who have not previously registered on the platform. Self-referrals or creating multiple accounts to earn referral bonuses are strictly prohibited and will result in account termination and forfeiture of all balances. Referral bonuses appear in transaction history as incoming transactions.
17. Engagement Games (Spin, Trivia, Login Streaks)
Wallet Masters offers engagement-based earning features including Spin the Wheel, Trivia questions, and Daily Login Streaks. Rewards from these features follow a plateauing progression system — early rewards are larger and decrease as users engage more, ensuring fair distribution. Earnings from engagement games are subject to platform limits and may be adjusted at any time. Automated tools, bots, or scripts to manipulate engagement features are strictly prohibited. Users found exploiting these systems will have their rewards reversed and accounts suspended. Engagement rewards are credited to the main balance and are subject to withdrawal limits.
18. TP$ Tap-to-Earn
The TP$ Tap-to-Earn feature allows eligible users (100,000+ USDT balance) to earn USDT by tapping on the screen. Earnings accumulate per tap with a multiplier system. A minimum of 1,000 USDT must be accumulated before transferring earnings to the main balance. TP$ earnings are subject to daily caps and platform limits. Automated tapping tools, scripts, or multi-account usage to inflate earnings are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account termination.
