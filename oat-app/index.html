How OAT Trades Works

1. Register. Create your account with your full name and the UID of the person who invited you. You receive your own OAT Trades UID.

2. Deposit. Fund your account with USDT, BTC or ETH. Deposits are verified by the support team and credited to your balance in USDT.

3. Trade. Choose an asset, invest 500 USDT or more, and the optimization algorithm trades for exactly 24 hours.

4. Double. When the 24-hour session ends, your total return is double your investment (100% profit). Cash out to your balance.

5. Team earnings. Every person you invite with your UID automatically earns 5% of your trading profit on every trade you complete — credited instantly to their balance.

6. Withdraw. Withdraw your funds and profits free of charge to USDT, BTC or ETH. Minimum withdrawal is 500 USDT.

Terms & Conditions

1. You must be invited by an existing member to use OAT Trades. The inviter's UID is required at registration and cannot be changed.

2. One active trade at a time per account. Trades run exactly 24 hours and return a fixed 2x payout.

3. Balances are denominated in USDT. Deposits in BTC or ETH are converted to USDT at the live market rate when verified.

4. Minimum trade amount is 500 USDT. Minimum withdrawal is 500 USDT. Withdrawals are free of platform fees.

5. Team earnings of 5% of trading profit are paid to accounts registered under your UID automatically when you complete a trade.

6. You are responsible for the accuracy of your withdrawal address. Transactions sent on the blockchain cannot be reversed.

7. Deposits, withdrawals and trades may be reviewed by the support team for verification and security purposes.

8. Sharing your UID gives others permission to join your trading team; it does not give access to your account.

9. Any attempt to manipulate the platform, duplicate accounts, or fraudulent deposits will result in account suspension.

10. Contact support in-app for any question regarding your account, deposits, withdrawals or trades.
